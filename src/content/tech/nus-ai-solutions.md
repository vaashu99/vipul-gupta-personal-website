From **6–8 July 2026**, I completed **Deploying and Operating AI Solutions** at
the National University of Singapore's Institute of Systems Science
(**NUS-ISS**). Receiving the Certificate of Completion was a welcome milestone
in an area I want to keep exploring: the engineering work behind dependable AI
systems.

## Connecting AI with operations

As an SRE, I'm interested in what happens after a demonstration works. I want to
understand how a service is deployed, how people investigate its behaviour, and
what makes it manageable over time. This course offered a focused opportunity to
look at those questions through an AI lens.

The official three-day curriculum covers how MLOps differs from traditional
DevOps, the pipelines around models and their operational data, and approaches
to scaling with cloud platforms. It also distinguishes MLOps from LLMOps and
their deployment strategies.
[See the NUS-ISS course overview](https://www.iss.nus.edu.sg/executive-education/course/detail/deploying-and-operating-ai-solutions/artificial-intelligence).

## The practical side of the learning

The hands-on work I shared in my learning update included running models locally
and deploying containerised APIs. I also explored LLMSecOps through prompt
testing for toxicity, bias and hallucinations, alongside observability work with
tracing and token and latency tracking.

That practical work gave me a starting point for thinking about reliability and security together,
while continuing to develop my understanding of AI operations.
[My learning update on LinkedIn](https://sg.linkedin.com/in/vipul-gupta-tech).

## Carrying the learning forward

For me, useful engineering starts with clear questions. What needs to be
observed? How will someone recognise a problem? What evidence should guide a
change? Those questions are familiar from SRE, and I want to keep asking them
when working with AI systems.

I see this course as part of that ongoing learning, with room to practise,
evaluate and improve. My aim is to apply the concepts thoughtfully to scalable,
reliable and secure AI systems. Thank you to the NUS-ISS instructors for the
learning experience.
